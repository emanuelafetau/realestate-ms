# Personas
## Real Estate Agency Management System

---

## Sara — Real Estate Agent

Sara, age 31, is a real estate agent at a property agency in Vlora, Albania. She was born in Berat and moved to Vlora at the age of 22 after completing a degree in Business Administration at the University of Tirana. She lives alone in a rented apartment close to the city center and has been with the agency for five years, starting as an administrative assistant before becoming a fully licensed agent in 2021. She is motivated by commission-based income and takes pride in being one of the top-performing agents on her team.

Sara manages around 20 active clients at any given time and handles everything from the first phone call to the final contract signing. Her days are rarely spent at her desk — she is usually out meeting clients, visiting properties, or attending viewings, and relies heavily on her phone to stay organised while on the move. She is comfortable with technology and uses her smartphone for most of her work, including emails, calendar management, and client communication.

Her biggest challenge is staying on top of follow-ups when she is juggling multiple clients at once. She currently takes notes on paper during client calls and often struggles to recall the details later. She loses leads not because she is a poor agent, but because there is no system keeping her accountable. She would benefit greatly from a tool that captures client information automatically, reminds her when to follow up, and keeps all her deals visible in one place so nothing slips through the cracks.

## Marco — Senior Real Estate Agent

Marco, age 44, is a senior real estate agent at the same agency in Vlora. Originally from Naples, Italy, he moved to Albania in 2010 after meeting his Albanian wife while travelling. He is married with two teenage children and has built his entire career in Albania through personal relationships and word of mouth. He has no formal qualifications in real estate but accumulated his knowledge through 15 years of hands-on experience and is widely respected by both colleagues and clients.

Marco works mainly with high-value properties and long-term clients — people who trust him specifically and return to him years later when they are ready to make their next move. He prefers phone calls over emails and still keeps a personal notebook where he writes client notes by hand. He is not a heavy technology user and tends to be skeptical of new systems, having seen several introduced at the agency over the years that made his job harder rather than easier.

His willingness to adopt the new system depends entirely on whether it makes his working life simpler. His most pressing need is a reliable way to remember long-term clients — people who are not ready to buy yet but will be in three or six months. He has lost several valuable clients simply because he forgot to call back at the right time. He is also not always aware of how long a property has been sitting without activity until the owner calls to complain. A system that handles reminders and flags stale listings would make a real difference to his work without requiring him to change the way he operates.

## Diana — Agency Manager

Diana, age 39, is the manager of the real estate agency in Vlora and is responsible for a team of eight agents. She was born and raised in Vlora and studied Economics at Tirana Business University before returning to her hometown to start her career. She is married with one child and is known within the agency for being organised, direct, and results-focused. She has been managing the team for six years and reports directly to the agency owner.

Diana is under constant pressure to deliver monthly revenue targets and is expected by the owner to have full visibility over team performance at all times. In practice, her current tools give her almost no real-time insight — she relies on agents to report their own activity, which is inconsistent, and only discovers problems when it is too late to fix them within the month. She is comfortable with technology and already uses spreadsheets and email extensively, but finds the manual process of pulling together performance data time-consuming and unreliable.

She sees the new system primarily as a management tool. What she needs most is a dashboard that shows her each agent's activity in real time so she can intervene early when someone is struggling. She also wants to formalise target-setting inside the system rather than communicating goals verbally in meetings. At the end of each month she currently spends two full days compiling a revenue report for the owner, and she is keen to have this generated automatically so she can focus her time on actually managing her team.

## Lisa — System Administrator

Lisa, age 35, is the office administrator at the agency and the person responsible for managing its internal systems and staff accounts. She was born in Tirana and studied Information Systems at a technical college before moving to Vlora in 2015 for work. She is single, lives close to the office, and has been with the agency for seven years. She is methodical, detail-oriented, and the first person anyone calls when something technical needs setting up or goes wrong.

Lisa does not work directly with clients or properties but plays a critical behind-the-scenes role in keeping the agency running. She manages all staff accounts, sets up access permissions, and handles the onboarding of new employees. She is highly proficient with office software and internal systems and takes her responsibility for data security and accountability seriously.

Her main concerns with the current setup are efficiency and traceability. Onboarding a new agent currently takes her most of a morning — setting up email, explaining multiple tools, handing over passwords — and even then the new agent often does not know where to start. She wants a system where creating an account and assigning a role handles everything automatically. She is equally concerned about offboarding — when an agent leaves, she needs to be absolutely certain that their access is revoked immediately and completely. She also needs a full audit trail of every action taken in the system so that if a client record is changed incorrectly or a document goes missing, she can trace exactly what happened and who was responsible.

## James — Property Buyer

James, age 34, is a software engineer originally from Bristol, UK, who relocated to Tirana in early 2025 for a position at a technology company. He is single, rents a flat in the city center, and has decided to buy his first property in Albania after settling into his new life there. He has never been through the Albanian property buying process before and is navigating it largely without local knowledge or family support nearby.

James is highly tech-savvy and does most of his research online before making any decision. He is methodical and likes to feel in control of processes, which makes the opacity of the local property market particularly frustrating for him. He does not speak Albanian fluently and depends on his agent to translate not just language but also local procedures and norms.

His relationship with the system is indirect — he interacts with it primarily through the notifications and communications it generates rather than by logging in himself. His biggest concern is being kept in the dark. He has heard stories from other expats about offers sitting unacknowledged for weeks and contracts delayed without explanation. He wants to receive updates automatically every time something changes with his offer or viewing, so he never has to chase his agent for information. He also wants to feel confident that the properties he is being shown have been genuinely matched to his stated preferences, rather than being random suggestions that waste his time and erode his trust in the process.
